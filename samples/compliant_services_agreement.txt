MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into between Northwind Analytics Pvt. Ltd. ("Supplier") and Harbor Retail Ltd. ("Customer").

1. Services. Supplier shall provide the data analytics services described in each statement of work signed by both parties. Each statement of work forms part of this Agreement.

2. Fees and Payment. Customer shall pay the fees stated in the applicable statement of work within thirty (30) days of receiving a correct invoice. Late payments accrue interest at one percent per month.

3. Confidentiality. Each party (the "Receiving Party") agrees to hold in strict confidence all Confidential Information disclosed by the other party (the "Disclosing Party"), to use it only for the purpose of this Agreement, and not to disclose it to any third party without the Disclosing Party's prior written consent, except to its employees and advisers who need to know it and are bound by equivalent obligations. These obligations apply equally to both parties and survive for five (5) years after termination of this Agreement.

4. Intellectual Property. Each party retains ownership of its pre-existing materials. Deliverables created specifically for Customer and paid for in full are owned by Customer, subject to Supplier's rights in its underlying tools and methods.

5. Indemnification and Limitation of Liability. Each party shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the other party against third-party claims, damages and expenses arising from its breach of this Agreement or its negligence. The total aggregate liability of either party under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim, and neither party shall be liable for indirect or consequential damages, including loss of profits.

6. Term and Termination. This Agreement begins on the effective date and continues for two years. Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving the other party not less than thirty (30) days' prior written notice. Either party may terminate immediately by written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and does not cure the breach within fifteen (15) days after written notice of the breach.

7. Data Protection. Each party shall comply with the data protection laws that apply to the personal data processed under this Agreement and shall keep such data secure.

8. Non-Solicitation. Neither party shall actively solicit for employment the other party's staff who worked on the services during the term of this Agreement and for six (6) months afterwards. General advertisements do not breach this clause.

9. Governing Law and Jurisdiction. This Agreement shall be governed by and interpreted in accordance with the laws of India. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts at Bengaluru, Karnataka.

10. General. This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both parties.
